What is an Image Compressor?

Our Image Compressor reduces photo file sizes (JPG, PNG, WEBP) without quality loss by optimizing color encoding and stripping unnecessary EXIF data.

How to use Image Compressor

  1. Provide Media File: Drag and drop your image or click to select a file.
  2. Select Compression Settings: Adjust quality level and export resolution.
  3. Save Locally: Click "Download Compressed Image" to retrieve your file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image without losing quality?
Setting the quality slider around 80% yields significant file size savings while maintaining visual fidelity.
Does this tool upload my images to a server?
No. All pixel operations occur strictly within your device memory.
