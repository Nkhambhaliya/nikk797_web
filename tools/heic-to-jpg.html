What is a HEIC to JPG Converter?

Our HEIC to JPG Converter converts Apple's High-Efficiency Image Container files into universal JPG photos right inside your browser without server transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC format?
HEIC (High-Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's standard photo format introduced in iOS 11. It provides high visual quality with lower storage overhead than JPEG.
Are my personal photos uploaded to a server?
No. All decoding and rendering execution is isolated strictly within your local browser sandbox.
