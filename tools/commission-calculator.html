What is a Commission Calculator?

Our Commission Calculator helps you figure out your cut from any transaction. Enter your gross deal size and percentage structure to compute your payout.

How to use the Commission Calculator

  1. Select your Currency: Choose your currency preference (USD, EUR, INR, etc.).
  2. Enter Total Sales Amount: Input the gross transaction total.
  3. Input Commission Percentage: Enter your agreed commission rate percentage.
  4. Calculate Results: Click "Calculate Earnings" to review your payout.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard commission rate?
Commission structures vary widely by industry. Tech and software sales typically command 10% to 15%, while high-volume retail or wholesale margins range from 3% to 6%.
Are commission payouts taxed?
Yes. Revenue authorities classify commission earnings as standard taxable income subject to standard income tax schedules.
Can I use this for affiliate marketing?
Yes. Simply enter your total driven sales volume into the Sales Amount field and your affiliate split rate in the Percentage field.
