What is an Overtime Calculator?

Our Overtime Calculator helps you quickly figure out exactly how much extra money you earned by working past your standard hours. Tap in your hourly wage and extra hours, and the calculator instantly projects your added earnings.

How to use the Overtime Calculator

  1. Select your Currency: Pick the currency you get paid in (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.).
  2. Enter your Hourly Wage: Type in your rate per hour.
  3. Input Overtime Hours Worked: Enter the number of extra hours worked.
  4. Set the Multiplier: By default, this is 1.5 (time-and-a-half). Adjust to 2.0 for double-time.
  5. Calculate: Click "Calculate Overtime Pay" to see your overall extra earnings instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard overtime multiplier?
Standard overtime is typically "time and a half" (1.5x your hourly pay). Holidays or extended shifts can require double time (2.0x) depending on labor regulations.
How is overtime pay calculated?
Formula: Hourly Wage × Overtime Hours × Overtime Multiplier. The resulting amount represents your added gross earnings.
Do salaried workers get overtime?
This depends on employment classification. Non-exempt salaried workers are legally entitled to overtime, whereas exempt employees typically receive a fixed wage regardless of hours.
Is overtime pay taxed?
Yes. Overtime pay is treated as standard earnings and subject to applicable tax withholdings.
